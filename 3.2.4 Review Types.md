[[Review type]] range form informal to formal. The level of formality depends on the [[SDLC]], maturity of the development process, criticallity and complexity of the work product being reviewed.
The same product can be reviewed with different [[Review type]]

Selecting the right [[Review type]] is key to achieving the required review objectives, the selection is also based on project needs, resources, work product type and risks.

[[Review type]] - [[Informal Review]] - Does not follow a defined process and does not require a formal documented output. Main objective is detecting anomalies.
[[Review type]] - [[Walkthrough]] - Lead by [[Author]]. Evaluates quality and builds confidence in the work product. [[Reviewer]]s might perform an individual review before, but is not required
[[Review type]] - [[Technical Review]] - Performed by technically qualified reviewers, Lead by [[Moderator]]. Gain consensus and make decisions regarding a technical problem, also detects anomalies
[[Review type]] - [[Inspection]] - Most formal type of review. Main objective is to find maximum number of anomalies. Other objectives are evaluate quality, build confidence in the work product. Metrics are collected. [[Author]] cannot act as the [[Review Leader]] or [[Scribe]]
