[[Test metrics]] show progress against the planned test schedule and budget, quality of the [[test object]], and effectiveness if the [[Test activities]] against [[test objectives]]

[[Test Monitoring]] gathers metrics to support [[Test control]] and [[test completion]]

Common [[Test metrics]]: Project progress, Test Progress, Product quality, defect, risk, coverage, cost
