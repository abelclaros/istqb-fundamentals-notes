[[Error guessing]] is used to anticipate the occurrence of errors, defects, and failures, based on the tester's knowledge (how the app worked in the past, type of errors devs tend to make, types of failures that have occurred in other similar apps)

[[Fault attacks]] are a way to implement [[Error guessing]], it requires to create or acquire a list of possible errors, defects, and failures, and to design tests that will identify defects associated with errors, expose the defects, or cause the failures
