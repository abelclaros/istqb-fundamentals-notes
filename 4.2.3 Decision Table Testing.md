[[Decision tables]] are an effective way of recording complex logic, such as business rules.
Conditions and resulting actions of the system form the rows fo the table
Each column corresponds to a decision rule that defines a unique combination of conditions, along with associated actions
To reach 100% coverage, test cases must exercise all these columns.
[[Coverage]] is measured as the number of exercised columns, divided by the total number of feasible columns

[[Decision tables]] provides systematic approach to identify all the combinations of conditions.
[[Decision tables]] help find any gaps or contradictions in the [[requirements]]
A minimized table or a risk-based approach may be used
