Benefit of [[test automation]] - time saved by reducing repetitive manual work
Benefit of [[test automation]] - Prevention of simple human errors
Benefit of [[test automation]] - More objective assessment and providing measures
Benefit of [[test automation]] - Easier access to information
Benefit of [[test automation]] - Reduced test execution times to provide earlier defect detection
Benefit of [[test automation]] - Faster feedback
Benefit of [[test automation]] - Faster time to market
Benefit of [[test automation]] - More time for testers to design new deeper and more effective tests

Risk of [[test automation]] - unrealistic expectations about the benefits of a tool
Risk of [[test automation]] - Inaccurate estimations of time, costs, effort to maintain and change existing manual test processes
Risk of [[test automation]] - Using a test tool when manual testing is more appropriate
Risk of [[test automation]] - Relying on a tool too much
Risk of [[test automation]] - Dependency on a tool vendor
Risk of [[test automation]] - Using an open-source software which may be abandoned
Risk of [[test automation]] - The automation tool is not compatible
Risk of [[test automation]] - Choosing an unsuitable tool that did not comply with the regulatory requirements
