 [[Defect management]] process includes a workflow for handling defects or anomalies from discovery to closure

[[defect reports]] objective - Provide information to resolve the issue
[[defect reports]] objective - Provide means of tracking the quality of the work product
[[defect reports]] objective - Provide ideas of improvement

[[defect reports]] include: ID, Title, Date, author, role, [[test object]], [[test environment]] ID, context of the [[defect]], steps to reproduce/proofs, Expected Results-Actual Results, Severity of the defect, priority to fix, status of the defect, References
