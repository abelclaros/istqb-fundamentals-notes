---
KnowledgeLevel: "K2: Understand"
Objective: Distinguish between project risks and product risks
Subtitle: "5.2"
---
[[Project risks]] are related to management and control of the project (organizational issues, people issues, technical issues, supplier issues)
[[Project risks]] when they occur affect the the project's ability to achieve its objectives

[[Product risks]] are related to product quality characteristics (missing functionality, poor architecture, security errors)
[[Product risks]] when they occur cause user dissatisfaction, loss of revenue, trust, reputation
