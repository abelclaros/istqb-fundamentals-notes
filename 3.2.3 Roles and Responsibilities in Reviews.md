[[Reviews]] involve various stakeholders, who can take on several roles.

[[Review roles]] - [[Manager]] - Decides what is to be reviewed, also provides resources (staff, time)
[[Review roles]] - [[Author]] - Creates and fixes the work product under review
[[Review roles]] - [[Moderator]] - (Facilitator) Mediation, time management, safe environment
[[Review roles]] - [[Scribe]] - (Recorder) collates anomalies and records review information, decisions and new anomalies found during review meeting
[[Review roles]] - [[Reviewer]] - Perform reviews. Can be someone that works on the project, an expert, or any other stakeholder
[[Review roles]] - [[Review Leader]] - Takes responsibility for the review such as deciding who will be involved, and organizing when and where
