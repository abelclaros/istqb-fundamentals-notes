[[Iteration planning]] -> Single Iteration
[[Release planning]] -> Release of a product

[[Release planning]] defines and re-defines the [[product backlog]] and refines large user stories in smaller ones
[[Release planning]] serves ad the basis for [[test approach]]
Testers in [[Release planning]] write testable user stories and [[Acceptance Criteria]]
Testers in [[Release planning]] participate in quality risk analysis
Testers in [[Release planning]] estimate test effort
Testers in [[Release planning]] determine the [[test approach]]
Testers in [[Release planning]] plan the testing for the release

[[Iteration planning]] is concerned with the iteration backlog
Testers in [[Iteration planning]] participate in the detailed risk analysis of [[user stories]]
Testers in [[Iteration planning]] determine the testability of user stories
Testers in [[Iteration planning]] breakdown [[user stories]] into testing tasks
Testers in [[Iteration planning]] estimate test effort
Testers in [[Iteration planning]] identify and refine functional and non-functional aspects of the test object
