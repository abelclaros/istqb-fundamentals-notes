# Introduction
[[Risk management]] increases the likelihood of achieving objectives, improve quality, and increase stakeholder's confidence and trust

[[Risk analysis]] consists of [[risk identification]] and [[risk assessment]]
[[Risk control]] consists of [[risk mitigation]] and [[risk monitoring]]

 the [[test approach]] based on [[Risk analysis]] and [[Risk control]] is called [[risk-based testing]]
 
```dataview
TABLE  WITHOUT ID
Title,
file.frontmatter.Objective AS Objective,
file.frontmatter.KnowledgeLevel AS KnowledgeLevel

WHERE contains(file.folder, this.file.folder)
WHERE contains(file.frontmatter.Subtitle, "5.2")
FLATTEN "[[" + file.path + "|" + truncate(file.name, 100) + "]]" as Title 
LIMIT 30
SORT Title ASC
```
