[[Review Process Activities]] - [[Planning]] - Defines the work product to be reviewed
[[Review Process Activities]] - [[Planning]] - Defines the quality characteristics to be evaluated
[[Review Process Activities]] - [[Planning]] - Defines the areas to focus on
[[Review Process Activities]] - [[Planning]] - Defines the exit criteria
[[Review Process Activities]] - [[Planning]] - Defines the supporting information such as standards, effort and the timeframes for the review

[[Review Process Activities]] - [[Review initiation]] - Main goal is to make sure that everyone and everything involved is prepared to start the review. This includes making sure that every participant has access to the work product under review, understands their role and responsibilities and receives everything needed to perform the review.

[[Review Process Activities]] - [[Individual review]] - Every reviewer performs an individual review to assess the quality of the work product under review, and to identify anomalies, recommendations, and questions by applying one or more review techniques (e.g., checklist-based reviewing, scenario-based reviewing). 

[[Review Process Activities]] - [[Communication and analysis]] - Found anomalies are analyzed and discussed. 
[[Review Process Activities]] - [[Communication and analysis]] - For every anomaly, the decision should be made on its status, ownership and required actions. This is typically done in a review meeting, during which the participants also decide what the quality level of reviewed work product is and what follow-up actions are required. A follow-up review may be required to complete actions.

[[Review Process Activities]] - [[Fixing and reporting]] - For every defect, a defect report should be created so that corrective actions can be followed up. Once the exit criteria are reached, the work product can be accepted. The review results are reported.
