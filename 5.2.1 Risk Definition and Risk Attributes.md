[[Risk]] is a potential event, hazard, threat whose occurrence causes and adverse effect.

[[risk characterization]] factor: [[Risk likelihood]] probability of occurrence
[[risk characterization]] factor: [[Risk impact]] consequences of the occurrence

[[Risk likelihood]] and [[Risk impact]] express the [[risk levels]], the higher the [[risk levels]] the more important is the treatment
