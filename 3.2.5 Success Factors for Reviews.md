[[Reviews]] - Success factor - Provide feedback from reviews to stakeholders and authors
[[Reviews]] - Success factor - Provide adequate time to participants to prepare for the review
[[Reviews]] - Success factor - Support from management
[[Reviews]] - Success factor - Making reviews part of the organization's culture
[[Reviews]] - Success factor - Provide adequate training for al participants so they know how to fulfill their role
