[[Test pyramid]] Tests in bottom layer are small, isolated, fast, and check a small piece of functionality
[[Test pyramid]] Top layer are complex, high-level, end-to-end test 

[[Test pyramid]] is a model that shows that different tests may have different granularity.
[[Test pyramid]] The higher the layer, the lower the granularity.
[[Test pyramid]] The lower the test isolation, the higher the test execution time
