Objective: Compare and contrast entry criteria and exit criteria
Subtitle: "5.1"
---
[[entry criteria]] defines preconditions to start an activity
[[exit criteria]] defines what to achieve to declare an activity complete

[[entry criteria]] and [[exit criteria]] should be defined for each [[Test levels]] and differ based on [[test objectives]]

[[entry criteria]] include availability of resources (tools, people, time, etc.), availability of [[testware]] (test basis, testable requirements, user stories), and initial quality level of a test object (smoke tests passed)

[[exit criteria]] include measure of thoroughness (numbers, statistics), and binary "yes/no" criteria (check list of tasks)

Running out of time is a valid [[exit criteria]] is stake holders are good with it

In agile, [[exit criteria]] is called [[Definition of Done]]; [[entry criteria]] is called [[Definition of Read]]
