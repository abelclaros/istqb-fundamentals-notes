[[Test reporting]] summarizes and communicates test information during and after testing.

During [[Test Monitoring]] and [[Test control]], the test team generates [[Test progress]] reports, those are generated in a regular basis (daily, weekly, etc.)

[[Test progress]] reports support the [[Test control]]  and provides information to make modifications to [[test schedule]], resources, or [[test plan]]
[[Test progress]] reports include: Testing period, [[Test progress]], impediments, [[Test metrics]], new and changed risks, testing planned

[[test completion]] reports summarize a specific test activity 
[[test completion]] report is prepared ideally when [[exit criteria]] has been met ([[Test levels]] completed, [[Test Type]] completed)
[[test completion]] report uses [[Test progress]] reports and other data
[[Test completion]] report include: [[test summary]], testing and product quality evaluation, deviations, testing impediments, [[Test metrics]], unmitigated risks, lessons learned

[[Test progress]] reporting can be informal or formal, depending on the audience and the stage of the process
[[Test progress]] reporting to the same team is frequent and informal
[[test completion]] reporting occurs only once and it has a template
